Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains how Kelime Bulmaca, a mobile game (Android and iOS) developed by Rabia Okumuş, handles personal data.

1. Data we collect

The game does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress, including your player name and avatar (levels, stars, hints, gems, streak, settings), is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect from your device:

  • advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level),
  • device and app information and ad interactions (views, clicks).

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form before ads load and can accept or refuse personalised ads. On iPhone, the App Tracking Transparency permission is requested separately; if you decline, ads are still shown but not personalised. You can change your choice in the game settings or in your device settings.

How Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Rewarded ads and purchases

Watching an ad to earn hints, chest rewards or gems is optional. There are currently no real-money purchases in the game; if added, this policy will be updated and payments will be handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never receive your card details.

4. Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete all game data on your device with Settings → Reset progress or Profile → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app. For Google's advertising data, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

6. Security

Game data stays on your device; we run no servers. Advertising data is processed under Google's security measures.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page, with the date above.
